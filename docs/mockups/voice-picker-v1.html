How voices get sorted

GroupVoices on this Mac (English)Where the label comes from
LifelikeSamantha, Karen, Moira, Tessa (♀) · Daniel, Rishi (♂), plus any Enhanced or Premium voice you download latermacOS reports gender for these. Any Enhanced or Premium voice counts as Lifelike automatically.
ArtificialFlo, Grandma, Sandy, Shelley, Kathy (♀) · Eddy, Grandpa, Reed, Rocko, Albert, Fred, Junior, Ralph (♂)macOS reports no gender for most of these, so Rigel keeps a small built-in gender list.
Novelty / effectsBad News, Bahh, Bells, Boing, Bubbles, Cellos, Good News, Jester, Organ, Superstar, Trinoids, Whisper, Wobble, ZarvoxNo gender. Shown last (or hidden) and never mixed into ♀/♂.
